# Proposal Summary

This proposal requests funding to launch a public evaluation phase for **Yoki Arena**, a community-developed Yoki-based game built for the Astar ecosystem.

The objective is to allow the broader Astar community to test the application under real conditions, collect usage data and feedback, and evaluate whether the project should continue its development as:

* A future component of Yoki Arcade;
* A standalone community application;
* Or a project that should not move forward beyond the evaluation phase.

The application is almost fully developed and operational.

The project already exists and is deployed on Minato (Soneium Testnet) through:

* A Farcaster mini-app compatible with Startale App;
* A standalone web application.

Funding is requested to finalize the production-ready deployment on Soneium Mainnet, complete ecosystem integrations, support infrastructure costs, and conduct a community-wide evaluation phase.

The requested funding is intended to validate real-world adoption, gather community feedback, and determine whether Yoki Arena can become a sustainable ecosystem application.

# Background

When members of the community requested a continuation of the Yoki NFT series, it was communicated that such a project would need to be developed by an independent contributor rather than directly by the Astar team.

Following this discussion, a Community Council member approached me for my thoughts on the idea, and I volunteered to build the project.

Over the past several months, I have continuously developed Yoki Arena while sharing regular progress updates with the community.

Today, the application is almost fully functional and can already be tested on Minato (Soneium Testnet).

However, access through testnet environments remains limited for many community members. This proposal aims to make the game accessible to a broader audience through a public deployment on Soneium Mainnet.

# Project Description

Yoki Arena is a competitive strategy game built around Yoki-themed characters and equipment.

Players participate in arena battles by assembling teams and equipment configurations. Battles are entirely simulated by advanced artificial intelligence. 

The AI does not treat both sides equally: it accounts for the raw power of your Yoki, the bonuses granted by your equipment, but also your accumulated experience as an onmyoji and your tactical knowledge of the spirits and artifacts you have encountered. 

The deeper your mastery, the sharper your edge. 

Every action and result is transparent, traceable, and secured via blockchain: you remain the true owner of your Yoki and their equipment.

The evaluation phase will focus on:

* User onboarding experience;
* Smart contract and infrastructure stability;
* Community engagement;
* Gameplay balance;
* Product-market fit within the Astar ecosystem.

# Benefits to the Astar Ecosystem

### Extending the Yoki Ecosystem

Yoki Arena provides a community-driven continuation of the Yoki universe, helping maintain engagement among existing Yoki holders and community members after the original campaign.

### Demonstrating Community-Led Innovation

The project showcases how independent builders can create ecosystem applications inspired by Astar initiatives without requiring direct involvement from the core team.

### ASTR Utility Through Startale App

Yoki Arena demonstrates a practical use case for the ASTR token within a consumer-facing application accessible through Startale App.

Players use ASTR to mint NFTs, upgrade and evolve them, and participate in arena activities, creating direct utility beyond traditional staking or governance mechanisms.

By combining Startale App compatibility, simplified onboarding, and ASTR-powered gameplay mechanics, the project serves as a real-world experiment for how users can discover, access, and interact with ecosystem applications using ASTR as the primary token.

The ASTR used within the game could either be allocated to the Community Treasury or permanently burned.

The use of ASTR as the primary in-game token is expected to create increasing demand for ASTR on the Soneium blockchain while simultaneously reducing the circulating supply through the burn mechanism.

### Gathering Actionable Ecosystem Data

The evaluation phase will provide measurable information regarding:

* User onboarding behavior;
* Retention rates;
* Participation incentives;
* Gaming engagement patterns;
* Community demand for future ecosystem applications.

### Sustainable Token Utility

All ASTR spent within the evaluation environment will be burned, creating a direct utility mechanism while preventing inflationary reward structures.

### Open Ecosystem Experimentation

The proposal allows the Astar community to evaluate a near-complete product before larger investments are considered, reducing risk while encouraging innovation.

# Evaluation Phase Structure

A public evaluation version of Yoki Arena will be deployed on Soneium Mainnet and distributed through Startale App.

The testing environment will be clearly identified as a pre-production version through:

* A dedicated evaluation NFT collection;
* Visible evaluation banners throughout the application;
* Distribution through Startale App debug mode.

Participation will be limited to 1,000 players by arena.

Minting cost will be 10 ASTR.

All ASTR spent within the application during the evaluation phase will be burned.
