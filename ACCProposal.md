# Yoki Arena – Community Evaluation Phase Proposal

Hey Astar community!

I'm GuiGou, and I'm here to present this proposal aimed at making it easy for the entire community to test **Yoki Arena**.

## Background

To provide some context, when the community requested a continuation of the Yoki NFT series, it was initially stated that this could not be developed directly by the Astar team and would need to be implemented by someone with the technical capabilities to do so:
https://forum.astar.network/t/proposal-yoki-community-mini-game-challenge/9368/5

I then volunteered to take on the project. Over the past several months, I have developed the dApp while regularly sharing progress updates with the community.

Today, the mini-app is already fully developed, deployed, and functional. It can currently be tested through Farcaster Preview on Minato (Soneium testnet).

However, I understand that not everyone can easily access Minato or Farcaster Preview. That's why I would like to give the broader Astar community the opportunity to test Yoki Arena directly on **Soneium** and help determine the project's future direction: whether it should be integrated into Yoki Arcade, continue as a standalone application, or simply not move forward.

You can find the full proposal discussion here:
https://forum.astar.network/t/proposal-yoki-arena-community-evaluation-phase/9507

## Evaluation Phase Overview

A public evaluation version of **Yoki Arena** will be deployed on the **Soneium Mainnet** and made accessible through the **Startale App**.

The application will be clearly identified as a testing environment through:
- A dedicated evaluation NFT collection;
- Visible pre-production banners across NFTs and application interfaces;
- Access through Startale App debug mode to avoid confusion with officially released products.

The minting fee will be **10 ASTR**, and all ASTR spent within the application during the evaluation phase will be automatically burned.

## Arena Structure

Battles will take place across three different arenas.
Participation will be open to everyone but limited to **1,000 players**.

### Arena #1

- Configured as **1v1 combat with 3 equipment slots**
- Registration opens immediately after deployment;
- Registration remains open for 6 days;
- Matches are automatically resolved by the AI within 12 hours after registrations close.

### Arena #2

- Configured as **1v1 combat with 3 equipment slots**
- Registration opens on Day 7 after deployment;
- Registration remains open for 4 days.

### Arena #3

- Configured as **3v3 combat with 3 equipment slots**
- Registration opens on Day 7 after deployment;
- Registration remains open for 8 days.
